Turf column — live animation prototype

Press Play to run a scripted capture sequence. Watch the %s tick up & down in sync with the sweep bar (that bar stands in for the map's territory front), cards slide to new ranks as the counts cross, and the header/figure do a kinetic side-swap when a card lands on the opposite parity. Toggle the side-swap style.

territory sweep (map front)

Ticking — the % is derived from a fractional course count, so a capture climbs e.g. 40→41→42→43 as the sweep completes; the loser ticks down the same beat.

Reorder — fires the instant the interpolated counts cross, so it's timed to the front reaching the deciding cell.

Side-swap — sides follow the slot (always alternating); on a swap the header + figure slam across (overshoot) with a color streak. No fading.

0% — a player who loses their last course ticks to 0 and the whole card eases to muted.
